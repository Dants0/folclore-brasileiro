First release! / Primeira versão!

Nine creatures from Brazilian folklore, each with the rules of its legend:
- **Saci**: steals from your inventory, can't be hurt, caught only with the Sieve; his cap, and the Saci in a Bottle.
- **Curupira**: punishes deforestation, backward footprints, calmed with honey.
- **Boitatá**: puts out fires and hunts whoever sets the forest ablaze.
- **Headless Mule**: pull off her iron bridle to break the curse; the bridle speeds up your horse.
- **Iara**: don't look at her, or she pulls you into the river.
- **Boto**: saves drowning swimmers; at night, the young man in the white hat.
- **Cuca**: comes for whoever hasn't slept for three nights.
- **Lobisomem**: on full-moon nights; break the curse to find out who he was.
- **Mapinguari**: only its belly mouth is vulnerable; drops a brutal claw.

`/folclore <creature>` to call any of them, and `config/folclore.json` to tune how often they appear.
