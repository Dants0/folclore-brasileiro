**Nine creatures from Brazilian folklore come to Minecraft, and each one plays by the rules of its legend.** You can't kill the Saci with a sword, the Curupira punishes whoever destroys the forest, and if you stare at the Iara, she pulls you into the river.

*🇧🇷 Nove criaturas do folclore brasileiro no Minecraft, cada uma com as regras da sua lenda. Descrição em português mais abaixo.*

---

## 🌪️ Saci
A whirlwind comes spinning towards you... and a one-legged boy in a red cap **steals an item from your inventory** and hops away laughing. No weapon can touch him: every blow just sends him whirling off. Only a **Sieve (Peneira)** thrown over him catches him. He gives back what he took and leaves his **cap** behind: it makes you faster, and no Saci will ever rob you while you wear it.
- Hold a **glass bottle in your other hand** when you catch him and you get the **Saci in a Bottle**: five favours (a whirlwind that brings every loose item to your feet)... then he pops the cork and escapes.
- He also blows out torches and braids horses' manes into knots.

## 🔥 Curupira
The guardian of the forest, with flaming hair and **feet turned backwards**. Cut down too many trees or hunt too many wild animals and you will hear a whistle in the woods... and find **footprints pointing the wrong way**. Keep going and the Curupira comes for you, making you lose your way. Plant saplings and the forest forgives you; an **offering of honey** calms him down.

## 🐍 Boitatá
A great serpent of fire with huge shining eyes. It crawls across the fields at night and **puts out every fire** it passes. **Light a fire near the forest** and it comes after you. Drops the **Boitatá Eye** (night vision and fire resistance).

## 🐴 Headless Mule (Mula sem Cabeça)
Gallops through the night, with fire where her head should be (especially from Thursday to Friday!). Wear her out, then **pull off her iron bridle with your bare hand** to break the curse. The bridle makes your own horse much faster.

## 🧜 Iara
She sings in the rivers at night. **Don't look at her**: whoever looks is drawn into the water, and whoever reaches her is pulled to the bottom. Look away to break the spell. Drops the **Iara Scale** (breathe and swim like her).

## 🐬 Boto
By day, a pink river dolphin that **saves drowning swimmers**. By night, a young man in a white suit and a white hat who dances by the shore and gives you presents in exchange for a fish. Knock his hat off and see who he really is. The **Boto's Hat** lets you swim fast and hold your breath longer.

## 🐊 Cuca
The alligator-headed witch comes for whoever **hasn't slept for three nights**. She throws potions and casts a heavy sleep on you. Go to bed and she gives up.

## 🐺 Lobisomem
On **full-moon nights** a man turns into a wolf. Wound him enough and the curse breaks: it was a villager all along, and he remembers nothing.

## 🦍 Mapinguari
The stinking one-eyed giant of the Amazon jungle, with **a mouth in its belly**. Its hide shrugs off almost every blow: strike it **from the front, right in the belly mouth**. Drops the **Mapinguari Claw**, a brutal weapon.

### ⚙️ Commands and settings
- `/folclore <saci|curupira|boitata|mula|iara|boto|cuca|lobisomem|mapinguari>` brings that creature next to you (great for videos!). Creatures you call this way, or spawn with an egg, don't leave at sunrise.
- `/folclore desmatamento [value]` shows or sets your "forest debt" for the Curupira.
- `config/folclore.json`: how often each creature appears (0 turns it off).

### 📦 Requirements
Minecraft **26.3**, **Fabric Loader** and **Fabric API**. Required on both client and server.

### 🎥 Videos, modpacks, servers
Make videos and streams (monetised is fine), put it in modpacks and on servers. Please download it only from the official pages. ## 🇧🇷 Em português

**Nove criaturas do folclore brasileiro, cada uma com as regras da sua lenda:**
- **Saci**: rouba um item seu e foge rindo num redemoinho. Nenhuma arma acerta o Saci: só a **peneira** prende ele. Pegue com uma garrafa na outra mão e ele fica preso na **garrafa**, te devendo favores.
- **Curupira**: derrube árvores demais e você ouve o assobio e acha **pegadas ao contrário**. Ele vem atrás de quem destrói a mata. Mel acalma ele.
- **Boitatá**: a cobra de fogo apaga os incêndios e persegue quem **bota fogo na mata**.
- **Mula sem Cabeça**: galopa à noite, principalmente de quinta para sexta. Canse a Mula e **arranque o freio** com a mão.
- **Iara**: canta nos rios. **Não olhe para ela!**
- **Boto**: golfinho cor-de-rosa que salva quem se afoga. À noite, o moço de chapéu branco que dança na beira do rio.
- **Cuca**: vem pegar quem **não dorme há três noites**.
- **Lobisomem**: aparece na lua cheia. Fira ele e descubra quem era.
- **Mapinguari**: o gigante fedorento da Amazônia, com uma **boca na barriga**. Acerte de frente!

Comando `/folclore <criatura>` para chamar qualquer uma (ótimo para gravar vídeo). Requer Fabric API, Minecraft 26.3.
